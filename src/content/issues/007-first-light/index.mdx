## What shipped

Bond looks slick. The atmosphere works. The problem is how often the game seems more interested in showing you an expensive stunt than letting you pull one off.

The vehicle chases are the clearest offender: explosions, speed, spectacle, and precious little room to do things your way. [GameSpot's review](https://www.gamespot.com/reviews/007-first-light-review-youth-in-revolt/) also describes their rigid direction. A blockbuster budget doesn't make holding forward exciting.

## The annoying shit

The frustration is the stop-start freedom: tools and opportunities, then another heavily directed sequence. Let us improvise instead of babysitting the next prompt.

Bluffing past security sounds properly Bond. When it feels like another button prompt, the tension disappears.

## The verdict

There are some "free" stealth sections. Calling the entire game an unplayable movie would be bullshit. But if you want the freedom of Hitman throughout, the cinematic hand-holding is the catch.

Nice suit. Loosen the leash.
