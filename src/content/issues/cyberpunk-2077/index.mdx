*Retrospective launch assessment: original PC, PS4 and Xbox One releases, December 10–24, 2020. The base-console failure determines this verdict.*

## What shipped

Night City had atmosphere, character, and an appealing mix of shooting and hacking. There was a game worth wanting underneath the mess. That doesn't mean everyone received a game worth paying for.

On base PS4, [launch testing](https://www.gadgets360.com/games/reviews/cyberpunk-2077-review-ps4-xbox-one-price-size-gameplay-2338569) documented repeated crashes and performance that made ordinary play miserable. This wasn't just a funny floating cigarette.

## The annoying shit

The biggest problem was unreliable play on hardware they sold the game for. That's **−40 overall**. Smaller visual bugs and awkward menus don't get extra deductions. :PepeHands:

Within the grace window, [CDPR apologised](https://www.gematsu.com/2020/12/cd-projekt-red-apologizes-for-cyberpunk-2077-performance-issues) and pointed players toward larger January and February patches. A patch schedule isn't a working game.

## The verdict

Failed launch. No +25 for the vision before subtracting the bugs. Test the fucking thing before asking for full price.

**February 15, 2022:** [Free patch 1.5](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/media/news/cyberpunk-2077-now-updated-for-next-generation-consoles-free-trial-available/) brought further improvements and native PS5/Series versions. **No repair credit awarded here:** that announcement alone doesn't establish restored, reliable play on the original affected consoles. Phantom Liberty doesn't buy the launch a clean record either.
