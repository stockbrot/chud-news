*Retrospective PC launch assessment: May 19–June 2, 2015. This score doesn't claim the original console builds ran equally well.*

## What shipped

A dark-fantasy world where the people and choices matter as much as the monsters. The quests give you reasons to care beyond clearing another bloody map marker. That's the case for **+35**: the writing and world do more than merely deliver a functional RPG.

The [contemporary PC review](https://www.pcgamer.com/the-witcher-3-review/) also found it ran well across several configurations. This assessment is about that original PC release, not the expansions or the later graphics overhaul.

## The annoying shit

Geralt moves like he needs a little extra notice before changing direction. Combat targeting doesn't help. **−5 for clumsy movement and control**, the biggest minor complaint in this assessment. Inventory clutter gets a mention, not another bill.

[PC patch 1.04, released May 25](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=292030&enddate=1437242873&feed=steam_community_announcements), addressed stability, performance and several UI bugs inside the grace window. Routine maintenance earns no bonus, and its notes don't prove every annoyance disappeared.

## The verdict

Strong delivery, memorable writing, and an actual adventure. Some friction, not a failed launch. :Clap:

Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine don't inflate this score. The base game earns its own points.
