## What you get

Control Resonant trades third-person shooting and corridors for melee combat in an open city. Different game loop, same mind-bending weirdness. That's the reinvention [Remedy advertised](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=419178), and it lands.

Combat is dodging, timing, combo finishers and meter management. Double jumps, air dashes, hovering and wall-running make traversal feel like a superhero game.

## The annoying shit

Too many of the best mechanics and zones arrive in the final hours. The opening acts can feel like a punishing grind. Let us get to the good shit sooner.

## The verdict

Playable, with the core experience intact. Length is a value complaint, not evidence of missing promised content.

*Pacing reflects the reviewed build; a [launch-day hotfix](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/action/control-resonant-embarrasses-me-specifically-with-a-launch-day-hotfix-negating-my-main-review-complaint/) adjusted combat balance.*
