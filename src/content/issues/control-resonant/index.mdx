## What you get

Control trades third-person shooting and corridors for melee combat in a more open city. Different game loop, same mind-bending weirdness. That's the reinvention [Remedy advertised](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=419178), and it lands.

Combat is dodging, timing, combo finishers and meter management. Double jumps, air dashes, hovering and wall-running make traversal feel like a superhero game.

## The annoying shit

Too many of the best mechanics and zones arrive in the final hours. The opening acts can feel like a punishing grind. Let us get to the good shit sooner.

No photo mode at launch, either. Removed a feature the prequel had. But we haven't verified a launch promise for it, so it isn't getting a broken-promise penalty.

## Launch score: +23

**+25 for delivering the advertised reinvention, trimmed by 2 for uneven pacing.** Playable, with the core experience intact according to this review. Length is a value complaint, not evidence of missing promised content.
