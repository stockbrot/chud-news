## What shipped

The graphics mostly look current-gen. Then you spot lava that looks like melted pizza cheese. The animation is worse: we've seen indie games move better than this.

Combat feels shallow and over-scripted. Too often, the game seems to play itself while you babysit button prompts. We'd take **Ninja Gaiden II (2008)** over this combat any day. All those claws, so little bite.

The story sands the edge off Logan. The angry, dangerous bastard we wanted feels watered down. They kept the claws and misplaced the spine.

## Paid extras

The [PlayStation Store lists a $10 Digital Deluxe upgrade](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP9000-PPSA03671_00-WOLVERINEUPGRADE), and [Sony confirms exclusive Deluxe items](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=419039).

Full price, then another bill for extras. Suck it.
