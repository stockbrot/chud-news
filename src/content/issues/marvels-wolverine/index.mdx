## What shipped

The graphics mostly look current-gen. Then you spot lava that looks like melted pizza cheese. The animations are even worse, we've seen indie games move better than this.

Combat feels shallow and over-scripted. Too often, the game seems to play itself while you babysit button prompts. We'd take **Ninja Gaiden II (2008)** over this combat any day. All those claws, so little bite.

The story sands the edge off Logan. The angry, dangerous bastard we wanted feels watered down. They kept the claws and misplaced the spine.

## The verdict

Insomniac Games totally missed the mark. People who expected a badass gut slashing Wolverine, got Soy-verine instead. Story is all over the place. Buggy.
No one knows how they've managed to spend 300 million $.
